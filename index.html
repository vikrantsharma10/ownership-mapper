What you get

Two layers. Built for two different conversations.

Layer 01

The executive picture, for the people who set the ceiling.

A crisp diagnosis your VP or Director can read in three minutes — where the process is structurally constrained and what needs to change at the top.

  • Ownership concentration — where single points of failure sit
  • Cross-functional dependency map and where it breaks
  • The one structural fix that unlocks the most SLA recovery
  • What the leadership conversation needs to cover
Layer 02

The full gap breakdown, for the people doing the work.

A structured breakdown your process owner or ops lead can action immediately — without a consultant, without a workshop.

  • Full ownership map — every step tagged Direct / Cross-functional / External / Shared
  • Elapsed time versus actual task time gap per step
  • Where AI can reduce the gap — one specific suggestion per eligible step
  • Who owns the delay — Yours to Fix / Influence / Negotiate
  • Two to three directional fixes sequenced by gap size
